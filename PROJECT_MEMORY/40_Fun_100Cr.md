## Data bugs found while building this (both fixed)

1. **Unadjusted prices** (#58): downloader used auto_adjust=False, so every
   split/bonus printed as a fake -50/80% cliff. Downloader now defaults to
   adjusted OHLC.
2. **Corrupt outlier prints** (#58b): Yahoo occasionally emits a one-day
   50x close — fabricated a Rs 20.5L "peak" (from a Rs 42k portfolio) for
   exactly one day. load_all() now drops |daily move| > 50%.

Earlier sweep/WF tables used the raw files; their rankings are broadly
robust but exact numbers will shift slightly on adjusted data.

## Read

- On DAILY bars, even Rs 100cr consumes only a few % of turnover
  in liquid Nifty50 names -> impact barely dents CAGR.
- Scale kills INTRADAY strategies (huge turnover share), not this.
- What Rs 100cr still cannot buy here: colo, latency, team — i.e.,
  the actual HFT game. It buys capacity, not edge.

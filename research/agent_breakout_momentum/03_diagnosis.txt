3.1  WAS THE 'CAP VIOLATION' REAL?
================================================================================================
PortfolioConfig has TWO distinct limits and step 2 conflated them:
  n_positions = 22  -> how many names the TARGET book aims for at a rebalance
  max_names   = 30  -> hard cap on names CARRIED between rebalances
  The turnover budget blends toward the target, so dropped names decay rather
  than vanish; the engine deliberately allows 30 material positions for that.

  observed max names held on TEST : 30
  engine hard cap (max_names)     : 30
  bars exceeding max_names=30    : 0  -> NO VIOLATION -- step-2 flag was my own
  bars exceeding n_positions=22   : 86 (expected and permitted: these are carried-over names)

  CORRECTION: step 2 compared against 22 (the target), not 30 (the cap).

3.2  THE MECHANISM: the gate is too sparse to fill the book
================================================================================================
PortfolioConfig demands a 22-name, 12%-capped, 90%-invested book. The Donchian
gate only qualifies ~6-7% of symbol-bars, i.e. ~2-4 of 48 names on a typical day.
With a 12% single-name cap, N qualified names can supply at most 12%*N.

names flagged on each rebalance date (n=34 dates):
  mean 3.4   median 2   min 0   max 14
  distribution: 0 flagged: 7 dates, 1 flagged: 8 dates, 2 flagged: 5 dates, 3 flagged: 3 dates, 4 flagged: 2 dates, 5 flagged: 1 dates, 7 flagged: 2 dates, 8 flagged: 3 dates, 11 flagged: 1 dates, 12 flagged: 1 dates, 14 flagged: 1 dates

invested weight (mean of held row sums): 0.3004
  min 0.0000  median 0.2160  max 0.7948
  cash held on average: 70.0%

  -> the gate can never reach the 90% target. Even a PERFECT breakout signal
     would leave the book ~70% in cash, for structural reasons:
     sparse flags x a 12% hard single-name cap.

  the 12% cap binds when flags < 7.5: 28/34 rebalances had <7.5 names -> cap binding
  0 names flagged on 7/34 rebalances -> fully in cash

3.4  IS 'BEATING THE COIN FLIP' REAL?
================================================================================================
  strategy beat 11/20 random-signal controls on CAGR
  one-sided exact binomial p (H0: coin flip) = 0.412
  -> NOT significant at the 10% level.
  strategy CAGR percentile vs controls: 1th
  random control CAGR mean 0.96% sd 2.41%
  strategy Sharpe -0.62 vs control mean -0.88 sd 0.40

  CONTROL SHARPE IS NEGATIVE (-0.88) AND THE STRATEGY'S IS
  ALSO NEGATIVE (-0.62). Beating a set of losing
  coin flips while losing money is not evidence of an edge.
  This is the honest reading of the brief §6.5 control.
